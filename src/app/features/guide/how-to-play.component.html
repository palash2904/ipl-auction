New user guide

How to Play

Run a live IPL-style mega auction with friends, owners, budgets, bids, sold players, unsold recalls, and a final squad summary.

Start Setup
1

Create the franchises

Go to Setup and add 2 to 8 teams. Each franchise needs an owner name, franchise name, and logo. Every team begins with Rs 120 Cr.

2

Owners join the room

Open Join, pick your franchise, and enter as the owner. The room keeps everyone aligned when sync is enabled.

3

Bid in the live room

The current player appears in Live Room. Owners raise bids using their paddle controls. The highest valid bidder leads until the player is sold.

4

Watch purse and squad limits

Teams must stay inside the remaining purse, 25-player squad cap, and 8-overseas-player limit. Spend carefully so you can complete the squad.

5

Recall unsold players

Players who receive no winning bid move to Re-auction. Bring them back later when teams have clearer needs and budgets.

6

Review the squads

Use Teams during the auction to compare rosters and purse balances. When done, open Summary to review or export the final squads.

Quick rules

Win condition

Build the strongest squad without breaking budget or composition limits. There is no automatic winner, so the fun is in strategy, steals, and bragging rights.

Rs 120 Cr starting purse Maximum 25 players per squad Maximum 8 overseas players Unsold players can return later
